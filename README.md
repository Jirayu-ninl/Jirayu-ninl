#### Building at [Axyra](https://www.axyra.one/en)
 
*Instruments for the present:* small, focused tools that show one thing clearly and tell you how far to trust it.
 
| Product | What it is | Platform · Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Axyra Calendar**](https://www.axyra.one/en/calendar) | A calendar where color is the time left. Works on your existing Google and phone calendars, with sky, daylight and weather built in. | Android · Early access |
| [**Axyra Octave**](https://www.axyra.one/en/octave) | One set of notes seen from every angle: fretboard, keyboard, staff and circle of fifths, plus tuner, tempo and ear training. | Android · In development |
| [**Axyra Spectrum**](https://www.axyra.one/en/spectrum) | Real-time audio analyzer with an on-device C++ engine, from tuning and loudness to room acoustics. | Android · In development |
| [**Axion**](https://axion.axyra.one/) | Node-based workstation for time-series data: visualize, simulate and forecast in one versioned graph. | Desktop (Tauri + Rust) · In development |
| [**Meridian Terminal**](https://meridian.axyra.one/) | Market research models and a realtime charting terminal, with backtesting and a no-code strategy builder. | Web · Waitlist |
